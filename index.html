Course Modules & Schedule

LESSON 01 Feb 17 • 14:00

Discrete Dynamics

Introduction to modeling. We explore simple iterative maps and population growth models.

  • Fibonacci Sequence & Golden Ratio
  • Malthusian Growth (Explicit vs Numerical)
  • Logistic Growth Models
  • Case Studies: Senegal & USA Populations
Launch Notebook 01
LESSON 02 Feb 24 • 14:00

System Dynamics (Predator-Prey)

Interacting populations and feedback loops. Introduction to phase space analysis.

  • Lotka-Volterra Equations
  • Spruce Budworm Outbreak Model
  • The Pesticide Paradox
  • Phase Plane Analysis
Launch Notebook 02
LESSON 03 Mar 03 • 14:00

Epidemiology & Agents

Comparing equation-based models with agent-based simulations (ABM).

  • SIR Model (Susceptible-Infected-Recovered)
  • Reproductive Number ($R_0$)
  • Random Walks & 2D Agent Simulation
  • Disease spread in spatial environments

Cellular Automata

Emergent behavior from simple grid-based rules.

  • Conway's Game of Life
  • Forest Fire Simulation
  • Elementary Cellular Automata
  • Von Neumann Neighborhoods

Chaos & Fractals

The edge of stability. Analyzing sensitivity to initial conditions.

  • Bifurcation Diagrams
  • The Mandelbrot Set
  • Julia Sets
  • Cobweb Plots & Strange Attractors
Launch Notebook 05

Final Project Themes

Students will select one of the following domains for their capstone simulation project. Project proposals are due by Mar 24.

Agent Based Modeling

Advanced interactions between autonomous agents.

Allee Effect

Population density dependence and extinction thresholds.

Compartmental COVID

SEIR models adapted for pandemic scenarios.

Falling Sand / Granular

Simulating physics of granular flow.

Forest Fire Dynamics

Complex percolation and spread models.

Procedural Level Design

Cave generation using Cellular Automata.

Leslie Matrix

Age-structured population modeling (Czechia Data).
