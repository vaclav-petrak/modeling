# Modelling & Simulation

Course materials for **Modelling & Simulation**, Summer Semester 2025/26, CTU Faculty of Biomedical Engineering.

The course website is served from `index.html` and covers six lessons, from discrete difference equations to chaos theory, with a computational approach using Python.

## Course Structure

| Lesson | Topic | Date |
|--------|-------|------|
| 01 | Discrete Dynamics | February 20 |
| 02 | System Dynamics (Lotka-Volterra) | March 6 |
| 03 | Epidemiology & Agents (SIR models) | March 20 |
| 04 | Cellular Automata | April 17 |
| 05 | Chaos & Fractals | April 17 |
| 06 | Synthesis: Choosing, Validating & Communicating Models | May 8 |

Each lesson folder contains:
- LaTeX source and compiled PDF slides
- `notebooks/` — Jupyter notebooks (linked to Google Colab)
- `code/` — interactive web demos (where applicable)

## Grading

Grades are based on three independent outputs — no exam:

- **Journal Club Presentation** — 10–15 minute presentation of a research paper
- **Capstone Poster Presentation** — simulation project poster (A1 format, May 22)
- **Project Review Discussion** — individual walkthrough of capstone project code and results

## Capstone Projects

Students choose one of 15 simulation project themes, including: Lotka-Volterra predator-prey, SIR epidemic models, cellular automata fire spread, Nagel-Schreckenberg traffic simulation, Leslie matrix population dynamics, diffusion-limited aggregation, and more.

Project proposals are due **March 24**.
