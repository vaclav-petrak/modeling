Granular Materials Simulation

Simulating granular flow and particle interactions using cellular automata.

🕹️ Focus: Cellular Automata & Particle Systems Open in Colab

Introduction

Figure 1: Example of a granular falling sand simulation.

Granular materials, such as sand, behave like both solids and fluids, exhibiting unique flow patterns under different conditions. In this project, you will create a cellular automata-based simulation of granular flow, similar to the mechanics seen in the game Noita. This will allow you to explore how particles interact, settle, and flow under gravity.

Task

Implement basic particle behavior of falling particles that move downward and settle when they reach a stable position, and at least two more of the following features:

  • Material Diversity: Introduce different materials (sand, water, oil, lava, gas) with distinct behaviors—e.g., sand stacks, water flows, and gas disperses.
  • Erosion and Deposition: Allow particles to wear away or accumulate in different environments, creating dynamic terrain changes.
  • Wind and Fluid Currents: Add directional forces that influence how particles move, mimicking wind or underwater currents.
  • Complex Structures: Implement rigid bodies or platforms that interact with falling materials, creating realistic physics-like constraints.
  • Other: Another feature of similar complexity of your choice.

Discussion Points

In your final presentation or report, be sure to address the following:

  • Core Mechanics: Explain your implementation of the core particle behavior, including how you handled particle movement and settling mechanics.
  • Demonstration: Demonstrate your result using a GIF or video in your presentation.
  • Additional Features: Present the two additional features you chose to implement, discussing your approach and any challenges you encountered.
  • Emergent Behaviors: Show examples of emergent behaviors (if present) in your simulation, such as unique flow patterns or unexpected particle interactions.
  • Parameter Analysis: Analyze how different initial conditions or parameters affect the system's behavior.
  • Real-World Comparison: Compare your simulation's behavior to real-world granular materials and discuss.
  • Literature Review: Compare your simulation to state-of-the-art research literature.
  • Reflection: Share what you learned about cellular automata and particle systems through this project.
